Client-acquisition rules

  • Every week must include at least one piece of content that makes the Elite Age Reversal Programme understandable and desirable.
  • Use the AFT audience and book as the top of the funnel; move serious enquiries into direct conversation and the client-leads pipeline.
  • Respond promptly to meaningful comments and DMs; record genuine prospects and their next action rather than leaving them buried in Instagram.
  • Balance reach content with proof and offers. Views without book clicks, enquiries or qualified followers are not the final objective.
  • Track client delivery separately from acquisition, but surface urgent client actions in the daily AFT priorities.

Operating rules

  • Use actual live activity and current analytics; do not attempt to “catch up” with an expired calendar.
  • Check that audio plays before publishing and again immediately after the Reel goes live.
  • Use Trial Reels where available, then publish the main Reel and complete Story/Highlight distribution.
  • Alternate family, education, proof/book and commercial content rather than dumping one series consecutively.
  • Organic Instagram and TikTok growth remains the priority; paid book promotion stays parked until deliberately reactivated.
  • The daily brief is AFT-only: posting, tasks, reach, book performance, clients and client acquisition. Exclude general news, email monitoring, fintech, senior-people updates and generic check-ins.
