House rules for every post

  • AGING?F***THAT! logo in a consistent position on every video.
  • Hook must earn the first 2 to 3 seconds. Family hooks come from the strongest answer, not automatically from the repeated interview question.
  • Use real voice. Family content stays unscripted.
  • Primary CTA where appropriate: DM the word AGE. Book-led posts can use “Link in bio”.
  • Invite the featured child as an Instagram Collab, not just a tag, when they are happy to collaborate.
  • Reels are the primary post. Share the Reel to Stories after posting.
  • Keep proof claims source-labelled. Do not describe estimated measurements as lab verified.
