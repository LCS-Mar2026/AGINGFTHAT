Social Media Operating Plan

Lean production version | 6 September 2026

Objective: grow qualified audience, book sales, affiliate engagement and AFT coaching leads without turning content production into an admin job.

Active content threads

FACT OF THE DAY

Daily surprising, useful, actionable and defensible health fact. One main Reel + one Trial Reel only. Trial changes the opening hook; keep the body and CTA stable.

I RECOMMEND

Products Lee genuinely uses/recommends. Explain what it is, why it earns a place, and route to the curated affiliate list with disclosure.

10 TENETS

Short-form lessons from the book/framework. One idea per Reel, strong visual or spoken hook, practical takeaway.

INTERVIEWS

Gracie, Aiden and other strong conversations. Cut for a single self-contained idea rather than arbitrary chronology.

TALKING HEAD

Lee direct-to-camera. Written hook large on screen; learn one repeatable visual interruption at a time. Keep production deliberately simple.

Shortest production route

PHONE → CAPCUT → GALLERY QC → INSTAGRAM → TIKTOK.

Shoot/select source with clear voice. In CapCut remove dead air, add approved captions/branding and retain the clean master. Export 1080×1920. Check picture and voice in Gallery at beginning/middle/end. Upload the checked file to Instagram; add quiet music only if it does not compete with speech. Cross-post the clean master to TikTok, never an IG-watermarked version.

Publishing & CTA

Reel: immediate hook → useful content → one action/insight → concise CTA.

Standard CTA: 📘 Get the Book · 🛒 AFT Recommended Products · 🔗 Links in bio & caption.

Book: https://amzn.to/4deKLyv
Recommended products: https://amzn.to/4iHOZCo

As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.

Share relevant main Reels to Story with the most useful tappable link sticker. Keep QR codes for print, desktop, events or genuine second-screen use.

Naming

Use the Brand Guidelines naming standard so files are recognisable in Samsung Gallery, CapCut and Instagram:

SERIES_###_TOPIC_MASTER_FINAL.mp4
SERIES_###_TOPIC_TRIAL_H01_FINAL.mp4

Codes: FOD · IR · TEN · INT · TH.

Fact of the Day rule

Maintain one canonical 365-fact bank. A fact must be generally unknown, useful, important, actionable, surprising and scientifically defensible. The public hook leads with the surprising idea, not “Fact 001”. Internal numbering stays in the filename/tracker.

Testing: publish the approved main version and one meaningful Trial version. Do not create H01–H04 families unless a specific experiment warrants it.

Tracking

For each main/trial pair record: ID, series, topic, hook, publish date/time, main vs trial, 24h views, reach, watch/retention where available, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows and CTA outcome. Compare like with like; promote repeatable winners into future hooks.

Operating principle

Consistency beats complexity. Preserve the recognisable AFT look and CTA, but spend creative energy on the opening idea and visual interruption. One new production trick at a time.
