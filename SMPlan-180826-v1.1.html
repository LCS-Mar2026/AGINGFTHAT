Social Media Operating Plan

Lean production version | 6 September 2026

Objective: grow qualified audience, book sales, affiliate engagement and AFT coaching leads without turning content production into an admin job.

Active content threads

FACT OF THE DAY

Daily surprising, useful, actionable and defensible health fact. One pre-release Trial + one scheduled Main only. The Trial tests a future FOD before followers receive the Main.

I RECOMMEND

Products Lee genuinely uses/recommends. Explain what it is, why it earns a place, and route to the curated affiliate list with disclosure.

10 TENETS

Short-form lessons from the book/framework. One idea per Reel, strong visual or spoken hook, practical takeaway.

INTERVIEWS

Gracie, Aiden and other strong conversations. Cut for a single self-contained idea rather than arbitrary chronology.

TALKING HEAD

Lee direct-to-camera. Written hook large on screen; learn one repeatable visual interruption at a time. Keep production deliberately simple.

FOD trial-first schedule

TRIAL → REVIEW → MAIN. Example: Sunday Trial for Tuesday's FOD → Monday review Trial performance and make only a worthwhile adjustment → Tuesday publish the Main to followers. Never routinely create Trial derivatives after the Main has already been published. Avoid near-duplicate Trial content that Instagram is likely to treat as already-posted material.

Shortest production route

PHONE → CAPCUT → GALLERY QC → INSTAGRAM → TIKTOK.

Shoot/select source with clear voice. In CapCut remove dead air, add approved captions/branding and retain the clean master. Export 1080×1920. Check picture and voice in Gallery. Upload the checked file to Instagram; add quiet music only if it does not compete with speech. Cross-post the clean master to TikTok, never an IG-watermarked version.

Fact of the Day admission test

Maintain one canonical 365-fact bank. A fact must be generally unknown, useful, important, actionable, surprising and scientifically defensible. The public hook leads with the surprising idea, not the internal fact number.

Default experiment: exactly one meaningful Trial before release and one Main on the scheduled release day. More variants require a specific reason.

Tracking

For each Trial/Main pair record ID, topic, hook, publish date/time, 24h views, reach, retention where available, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows and CTA outcome. Use the Trial to inform the future Main; compare like with like.

Consistency beats complexity. Preserve the recognisable AFT look and CTA; spend creative energy on the opening idea and visual interruption.
