SOCIAL MEDIA · FACT OF THE DAY

ChatGPT FOD HD Video Production

Canonical AFT production logic | Established 9 September 2026

Purpose

This is the repeatable production standard for AGING? F***THAT! Fact of the Day videos. The objective is maximum visual and informational impact for likes, saves, follows and qualified website visits, while protecting scientific credibility.

1 · Evidence rule — non-negotiable

Every factual claim must be scientifically verifiable. A claim is not used merely because it is dramatic, plausible or viral.
Association remains associated with; never silently convert association into causation.
If evidence is disputed, uncertain or context-dependent, qualify the claim accurately or omit it.
Evidence checking applies to the hook, mechanism, three facts, interventions and claimed benefits.

2 · Audience + language

Write for intelligent, successful laypeople interested in age reversal, longevity and healthspan, but do not assume medical, nutrition or exercise knowledge. Explain terms such as insulin resistance, visceral fat, BDNF or osteoporosis in ordinary English before relying on them.

Voice: direct, surprising, useful and evidence-led. Make the harmful product, habit, misconception or biological process the villain where appropriate; never shame the viewer.

3 · Script architecture

NASTY MESSAGE / HOOK: one high-impact but defensible statement showing why the viewer should care.
HIDDEN MECHANISM: explain what is actually happening inside the body in layman's English.
3 VERIFIED FACTS: memorable, high-impact facts that materially improve understanding.
F*** THAT PIVOT: move from threat to agency.
3 SPECIFIC INTERVENTIONS: tell the viewer exactly what to do — frequency, duration, quantity or behaviour wherever evidence permits. Avoid vague advice such as “build muscle” or “move more”.
PAYOFF: connect the actions to capability, healthspan or biological aging without making unsupported age-reversal claims.

Target spoken duration: approximately 45–60 seconds. Preserve useful explanation rather than compressing the science into jargon.

4 · HD visual master

Format: Instagram Reel 9:16 vertical. Delivery master at least 1080 × 1920. Create the highest-quality source imagery available so the final 1080 × 1920 export remains crisp.

Look: premium cinematic biological/anatomical imagery; high detail, depth, strong definition, vibrant but controlled colour, realistic tissue/material rendering and deliberate focal hierarchy. It must not look like generic stock wellness art or low-resolution AI output.

Clean visual first: do not allow generative imagery to invent captions, labels or logos. Text and branding are controlled production layers.

5 · Motion standard

Create approximately 5–6 seconds of meaningful, loopable biological motion appropriate to the subject: flowing particles, blood/glucose movement, signalling pulses, organ/cellular activity, depth/parallax and restrained cinematic camera movement.

A still image with a simple zoom is not an acceptable FOD animation.

The short animation may be looped or reused beneath the longer voiceover/edit.

6 · AFT brand control

Use only approved AFT identity assets. Never generate or approximate the AGING? F***THAT! logo with AI. Use the actual approved PNG logo files and the correct standard/inverted and single-line/stacked version for the placement.

Canonical digital colours are defined in the Brand Guidelines. Current core references include AFT Brown #3D1C02, AFT Blue #A9CDEA, Ink #031522, Panel #061D2C and Luminous Cyan #2BC7FF.

The floating logo should be present on branded Reel masters without obscuring the biological subject or mobile-safe content area.

7 · ChatGPT → Canva production workflow

ChatGPT: topic research → evidence verification → script → visual concept → Ultra-HD source visual/animation → production instructions.

Canva: central AFT branded production and asset repository wherever connector capabilities support the operation. Apply approved logo assets, brand treatment and reusable Reel construction. Preserve clean source assets as well as branded masters.

Final edit/publish: voiceover, captions and final timing can be completed in the established editing workflow before Instagram publishing.

Do not default to sending production to an unrelated third-party AI video service when the required asset can be produced through the established ChatGPT/AFT workflow.

8 · Asset structure + naming

For each FOD preserve: (1) clean Ultra-HD source visual, (2) clean 5–6 second animation, (3) branded master, (4) final Reel/edit where applicable, and (5) verified final script.

FOD_###_TOPIC_MASTER_FINAL.mp4
FOD_###_TOPIC_TRIAL_H01_FINAL.mp4

Canva should act as the reusable visual-production library rather than allowing finished assets to remain scattered across chats and downloads.

9 · Daily production sequence

SELECT → VERIFY → SCRIPT → VISUALISE → GENERATE → ANIMATE → BRAND → REVIEW → EXPORT → TRIAL → REVIEW → MAIN.

Quality gate before export: science verified; hook defensible; three interventions specific; no invented text/logo; biological visual genuinely premium; motion meaningful; logo correct; mobile-safe 9:16 composition; clean master retained.

10 · Creative benchmark

The benchmark is the high-quality AFT biological animation standard established through the Lymph, VO₂ Max and Insulin Resistance development work: visually arresting enough to stop the scroll, understandable without specialist knowledge, scientifically defensible, and useful enough to save or share.
