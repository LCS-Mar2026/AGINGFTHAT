MEASURE → OPTIMISE → VERIFY

What we measure

The AFT Bio Age itself is calculated from 12 markers across 4 domains. Broader testing provides additional healthspan context and helps shape the programme; it does not change the definition of the 12-marker AFT Bio Age model.

🩸

AFT Customised Biomarker Blood Panel

A targeted panel selected for the AFT Bio Age model and broader healthspan assessment, rather than a generic “full blood panel”.

🦴

DEXA

Body fat, visceral fat, lean mass and bone density.

🫁

VO₂ Max

Cardiorespiratory fitness, aerobic capacity and fitness benchmarking.
