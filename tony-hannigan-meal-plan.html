Everyday structure

Choose one lunch and one dinner. Use an evening snack where necessary to make up the remaining approved daily macro allowance. Lisa can continue cooking the family's preferred dishes; Tony's portions, cooking oils, sauce ingredients, lean protein and vegetable servings are adjusted.

Nutrition targets pending reconciliation: The existing live portal's exact macro targets were not accessible during this revision. These are estimated, portioned versions and have not been verified as matching the current approved plan. Calorie estimates use 4 kcal/g for protein and carbohydrate, and 9 kcal/g for fat. Actual brands, cooked yields and recipes change the values. Do not describe these as final clinical prescriptions.

Important: Given Tony's cardiovascular history and prescribed treatment, have any substantial dietary change checked by his clinical team. No potassium-chloride salt substitutes or potassium supplements without a clinician reviewing medicines, kidney function and potassium. Keep added salt modest and follow any individual fluid advice.

Lisa's kitchen: keep the taste, adjust the portions

Keep: scratch-cooked sauces, aromatic curries, bolognese, roasts, chicken, fish, lean red meat, potatoes, sweet potatoes, rice and spaghetti. Use plenty of herbs, garlic, lemon, spices, fresh vegetables and slow cooking.

Modifications: choose leaner cuts, skinless chicken, reduced-fat yoghurt/ricotta, 5% mince; trim visible fats; measure oil, butter, cheese, cream, pesto and coconut milk. Use lower-salt stock and modest soy sauce. Potatoes and sweet potatoes are included deliberately.

Occasional rather than regular choices: giant Yorkshire & sausage, fish fingers, easy pizza, parmesan ham macaroni and sausage/chicken gumbo; these are on Lisa's list, but the original versions can be higher in sodium/saturated fat. They can be adapted and nutritionally calculated separately.
