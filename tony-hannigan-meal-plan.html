Lisa's kitchen: keep the taste, adjust the portions

Keep: scratch-cooked sauces, aromatic curries, bolognese, roasts, chicken, fish, lean red meat, potatoes, sweet potatoes, rice and spaghetti. Use plenty of herbs, garlic, lemon, spices, fresh vegetables and slow cooking.

Modifications: choose leaner cuts, skinless chicken, reduced-fat yoghurt/ricotta, 5% mince; trim visible fats; measure oil, butter, cheese, cream, pesto and coconut milk. Use lower-salt stock and modest soy sauce. Potatoes and sweet potatoes are included deliberately.

Occasional rather than regular choices: giant Yorkshire & sausage, fish fingers, easy pizza, parmesan ham macaroni and sausage/chicken gumbo; these are on Lisa's list, but the original versions can be higher in sodium/saturated fat. They can be adapted and nutritionally calculated separately.
