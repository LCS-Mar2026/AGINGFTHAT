Brand Guidelines

Private working standard | 6 September 2026

Core identity

Palette: Espresso #3D1C02 · Duck Egg #A9CDEA · White/Cream backgrounds. Keep layouts clean, high contrast and recognisable on a phone.

Voice: surprising, useful, evidence-led and direct. Make the product, habit or misconception the villain where appropriate; never shame the viewer.

Reel visual standard

9:16 vertical · 1080 × 1920 · hook visible immediately · captions legible on mobile · AFT identity present without obscuring the subject · one clear CTA. Talking-head videos use one deliberate visual interruption at a time while the written hook remains dominant.

Video naming

Every asset must remain recognisable in Gallery, CapCut and Instagram. Use:

SERIES_###_TOPIC_MASTER_FINAL.mp4
SERIES_###_TOPIC_TRIAL_H01_FINAL.mp4

Series codes: FOD Fact of the Day · IR I Recommend · TEN 10 Tenets · INT Interviews · TH Talking Head.

Publishing rule

Keep the clean CapCut master. Verify picture and voice in Gallery before upload. Instagram receives the clean export; TikTok never receives an Instagram-watermarked copy.

Fact of the Day: TRIAL → REVIEW → MAIN. Exactly one meaningful Trial is published before the scheduled Main. Review its performance and make only a worthwhile adjustment. Do not routinely publish post-Main Trial derivatives.
