Brand Guidelines

Private working standard | Website visual language updated 9 September 2026

Core identity

Voice: surprising, useful, evidence-led and direct. Make the product, habit or misconception the villain where appropriate; never shame the viewer.

Visual character: premium health-tech rather than conventional wellness. Dark biological/data environment, luminous cyan edges, strong contrast, disciplined typography and restrained AFT brown/copper accents.

WEBSITE · DASHBOARDS · DIGITAL

Canonical visual language

The current AFT homepage is the master reference for website styling. Carry this visual language consistently through public pages, client dashboards and admin-facing AFT interfaces.

Backgrounds: deep navy/black with radial blue depth rather than flat black or white panels.
Panels: dark blue glass/control-centre surfaces with fine cyan borders, rounded corners and restrained cyan glow.
Navigation and CTAs: icon-led, high contrast, consistent sizing and cyan edge treatment. Primary commercial CTAs may use AFT brown/copper inside the cyan system.
Imagery: rich, high-impact human performance, biological and data-led imagery. Never invent or substitute personal photographs when approved AFT assets already exist.
Dashboards: may intensify the same language with gauges, rings, trajectories and data panels, but must remain recognisably AFT.

TYPOGRAPHY

Font and hierarchy

Primary digital typeface: Arial, sans-serif. Use weight, scale, spacing and colour to create hierarchy rather than mixing typefaces.

H1 · PRIMARY STATEMENT
H2 · Section heading
H3 · Panel or subheading
Body copy · clear, compact and highly legible on mobile.

H1: very bold, tightly spaced, high-impact. Selective cyan emphasis is permitted.
H2: bold and clearly subordinate to H1.
H3/H4: structured panel and sub-navigation hierarchy.
Eyebrows/labels: uppercase, small, bold, increased letter spacing, luminous cyan.
Body: regular Arial with off-white primary text and muted blue secondary text.

Reel visual standard

9:16 vertical · 1080 × 1920 · hook visible immediately · captions legible on mobile · AFT identity present without obscuring the subject · one clear CTA. Talking-head videos use one deliberate visual interruption at a time while the written hook remains dominant.

Video naming

Every asset must remain recognisable in Gallery, CapCut and Instagram. Use:

SERIES_###_TOPIC_MASTER_FINAL.mp4
SERIES_###_TOPIC_TRIAL_H01_FINAL.mp4

Series codes: FOD Fact of the Day · IR I Recommend · TEN 10 Tenets · INT Interviews · TH Talking Head.

Publishing rule

Keep the clean CapCut master. Verify picture and voice in Gallery before upload. Instagram receives the clean export; TikTok never receives an Instagram-watermarked copy.

Fact of the Day: TRIAL → REVIEW → MAIN. Exactly one meaningful Trial is published before the scheduled Main. Review its performance and make only a worthwhile adjustment. Do not routinely publish post-Main Trial derivatives.
