// The Transformation

This Is What's Possible

"One hour of movement, dedicated to your longevity, every single day. That's the backbone. Everything else builds on it."

At 35, a back injury left Lee incapacitated for weeks at a time — spending over $1,000 per month just to function, facing the prospect of vertebral fusion surgery.

But, the real turning point came in 2015. A major life event that could have broken him instead drove him back to one non-negotiable rule: an hour of movement, dedicated to longevity, daily — walking, stretching, the gym, whatever the day called for.

Since 2015, Lee has reversed his biological age by nearly 20 years — down to 44, verified through regular DEXA scanning and blood biomarker tracking, built on nutrition, sleep, hormones, biomarkers, and now the frontier of therapeutic peptides.

It's not about looking good for your age. It's about enjoying your life with your friends and family for as long as possible — choosing the activities you want, not just the ones your body can still perform.

// The Framework

The 10 Tenets

These are not tips. They are a complete architecture for biological age reversal — built from two decades of personal practice, evidence-based medicine, and the latest longevity science. They work in combination. You don't pick the ones you like. You implement the system.

01
🎯
Mindset & Purpose
Everything starts with WHY. Without a North Star — a specific, visceral, future vision of yourself — every other tenet collapses under pressure. Lee's is snowboarding at 80. The first tenet rewires how you think about ageing, permanently.
→ Find your North Star first. Everything else follows.
02
🥗
Nutrition
Modified Mediterranean/Paleo. An 18:6 intermittent fasting window. Calorie deficit, low GI — not a rigid protocol, but a sustainable, evidence-aligned relationship with food that works with your biology rather than against it. Anti-inflammatory at its core.
→ Low carb eating reduces insulin resistance and accelerates fat metabolism.
03
💪
Movement
One hour of movement, dedicated to longevity, daily — without exception. HiiT, calisthenics, yoga, swimming, cycling, rowing, golf, tennis, padel, or simply a long walk. The form changes daily; the non-negotiable hour doesn't. 365 days a year since 2015 — this is the tenet that anchors everything else.
→ Daily movement can reverse cardiovascular age by up to 10 years.
04
😴
Sleep
7–8 hours. 528Hz frequency audio. Sleep environment optimisation. Circadian rhythm alignment. Sleep is where growth hormone peaks, tissue repairs, and the nervous system resets. You cannot biohack your way around chronically poor sleep.
→ Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep — poor sleep collapses it completely.
05
🧘
Stress Control
Chronic stress is one of the most potent accelerators of biological ageing known to medicine. It raises cortisol, shortens telomeres, destroys sleep, and kills motivation. Lee has lived this — the 2015 crisis proved it. This tenet gives you the complete architecture to dismantle it.
→ Chronic stress shortens telomeres — the biological marker of cellular age.
06
⚗️
Genetics & Hormones
The honest conversation mainstream medicine still doesn't have loudly enough. Understanding your genetic predispositions, optimising your hormonal environment, and using evidence-based interventions — TRT, peptides, targeted supplementation — without apology.
→ Testosterone decline is not inevitable — it is addressable.
07
💊
Supplements
Not a medicine cabinet of marketing. A curated, evidence-based stack — Omega-3, Vitamin D3/K2, Magnesium, NMN, Resveratrol, and targeted additions based on your own bloodwork. What you need, when you need it, in doses that actually move the dial.
→ Vitamin D deficiency accelerates biological ageing at the cellular level.
08
📊
Biohacking & Data
DEXA scans. Blood biomarker panels. HRV tracking. VO₂ max testing. Continuous glucose monitoring. If you're not measuring, you're guessing. This tenet turns your body into a data-driven system — and biological age into a number you actively manage.
→ VO₂ max is the single strongest predictor of longevity — and fully trainable.
09
🤝
Community & Connection
Loneliness is as damaging to health as smoking 15 cigarettes a day. Social connection, shared purpose, and genuine relationships are not soft extras — they are measurable contributors to healthspan and longevity. This tenet is the one most men over 50 get wrong.
→ Strong social bonds reduce all-cause mortality by up to 50%.
10
🏔️
Purpose & Legacy
The tenet that ties all nine together. People with a defined sense of purpose live measurably longer. This final tenet takes everything you've built — the body, the data, the habits — and anchors it to something that makes you want to be here at full capacity for as long as possible.
→ Ikigai — a reason for being — is directly correlated with longevity in population studies.
