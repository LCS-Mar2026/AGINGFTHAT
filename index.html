About Lee

THE MAN WHO
PROVED IT

"I spent decades getting it wrong. Now I've spent years getting it right — and I wrote the book so you don't have to waste as much time as I did."

Lee is a UK-based health, wellness and longevity expert, author and speaker. A former martial artist who suffered a serious back injury in his mid-30s, he spent years carrying 25–40lbs of excess weight, eating badly, drinking too much, and cycling in and out of fitness regimes that never stuck.

Then, in his 50s, something shifted. He reversed it all. No miracle pills. No fad diet. Just a systematic approach to the 10 things that actually move the needle on how you age — mentally and physically.

Now on TRT since March 2021, exercising daily, following a Modified Mediterranean/Paleo diet, and fasting 18:6, Lee is living proof that your biological clock isn't fixed. He's built the F***THAT! community around this truth — and his three children are all health and fitness models, so it clearly runs in the family.
