About Lee

THE MAN WHO
PROVED IT
WORKS

"I spent decades in a body I wasn't proud of. Then 2015 happened — and when I came out the other side, I had a system that changed everything."

Lee is a UK-based health, wellness and longevity expert. He practised martial arts until he was 15. In his 30s he suffered a debilitating back injury that left him incapacitated at least once a month for years — spending over $1,000 a month on physiotherapy, chiropractic and various treatments, and at his lowest point considering vertebral fusion surgery.

The turning point wasn't the injury. It was a period of significant personal and professional stress around 2015 that forced a complete rebuild. Lee returned to the one principle he'd always known worked: one hour of hard exercise, every single day, without negotiation. That was the backbone of everything that followed.

He now follows a 14:10 eating window, very low carb, high protein approach — not a rigid protocol, just the way he eats. His three children have all grown up with the same health and fitness focus embedded in their lives. The apple, it turns out, doesn't fall far from the tree.
