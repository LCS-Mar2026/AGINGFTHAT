THE EXPERIMENT IS PERSONAL

From midlife decline to measurable performance.

Lee C. Stonehouse built AGING? F***THAT! around a simple premise: don't guess about ageing. Measure what can change, intervene intelligently, then measure again.

The approach combines exercise, nutrition, recovery, body composition and biomarker tracking into one repeatable framework.
