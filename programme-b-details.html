PROGRAMME B · 12-MONTH HYBRID/VIRTUAL

12-Month Virtual

Programme B follows the same three-phase AFT methodology, without the Elite A 10-year guarantee or prescribed biological-age phase targets. Progress is assessed against the client's individual baseline, objectives, measured response and biometric outcomes.

Phase Graduation Reviews

Formal Graduation Reviews are conducted at Months 4, 8 and 12. Dashboard performance, biometric change, adherence and intervention response are assessed and used to establish the priorities for the succeeding phase or long-term strategy.
