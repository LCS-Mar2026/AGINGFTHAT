Bio Age Model

Tracks changes in modifiable physiological measures across 12 key biomarkers, body composition, fitness, blood and vital signs.

How it works. Independently verified biomarkers are input and weighted to calculate Bio Age. The chart shows the divergence between Bio Age and chronological age at the measured points. A Bio Age older than chronological age is shown in red; a younger Bio Age is shown in green. Divergence is capped at ±20 years. Any dotted extension of the Bio Age line is an extrapolation only.
