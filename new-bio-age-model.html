AFT Composite Biological Age Model

September 2026 · Working Model v1.1 · MODEL PAGE — NO PERSONAL DATA STORED HERE

This page defines the AFT programme-specific composite model: the markers, weights, scoring bands and calculation logic. It is designed to track change in modifiable physiological measures across the Age Reversal Programme. It is not an epigenetic clock, a validated clinical ageing clock, or a medical diagnosis.

How it works. Each entered marker is converted to a score of 100, 75, 40 or 10 using the bands below, then multiplied by its agreed model weight. Missing markers are excluded rather than estimated and the remaining weights are automatically re-normalised. The weighted composite is translated into an estimated age difference from chronological age, capped at ±20 years.

Calculation logic

marker score ∈ {100, 75, 40, 10}
weighted composite = Σ(score × weight) / Σ(weights of entered markers)
biological age = chronological age − ((weighted composite − 50) / 50 × 20), capped to chronological age ±20

Selection principle: markers are prioritised for relevance to the programme and meaningful reversibility over approximately 3–12 months. DEXA, blood testing and VO₂ max are intended for baseline, midpoint and end-of-programme comparison. Blood results should be clinically interpreted by the client's own laboratory/doctor.
