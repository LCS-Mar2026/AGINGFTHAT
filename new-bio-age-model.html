How This Number Is Built
The score combines nine markers across four categories: body composition (DEXA scan), cardiovascular fitness (VO2 max), blood chemistry, and vitals. Each marker is scored against age matched reference ranges and given a weighting based on how strongly it predicts biological aging and how responsive it is to lifestyle change. The nine markers carry a combined weight of 75 out of a possible 100 in the full model. Three markers (hsCRP, homocysteine, free testosterone) were not available from current records and are excluded rather than estimated. The result is a weighted average, not a simple mean, so the strongest predictors (VO2 max, HbA1c, visceral fat, lean mass) carry more influence than the others.

Notes and Limitations

Total testosterone dropped sharply against the prior reading (minus 12.1 nmol/L) while remaining in range. Worth confirming this isn't a lab or unit change before treating it as a trend.

VO2 max is a self administered rowing estimate, not a lab based maximal test. A supervised test is planned in Brighton in the coming weeks, and this figure should be updated once that is done.

hsCRP, homocysteine, and free testosterone were not available for this cycle and were excluded rather than guessed. Adding them would sharpen the estimate.

Cholesterol and LDL read out of range on the standard reference, consistent with the known pattern on a ketogenic diet and not treated as a negative marker here.

BMI was deliberately excluded as unreliable for a high muscle mass individual. Free testosterone weighting was reviewed and kept at 7.
