Bio Age Model

Tracks changes in modifiable physiological measures across 12 key biomarkers, body composition, fitness, blood and vital signs.

How it works. Independently verified biomarkers are input and weighted to calculate Bio Age. The chart plots chronological age on the horizontal axis and Bio Age on the vertical scale. The pale diagonal is the age-equality line: points above it are biologically older and points below it are biologically younger. Divergence is capped at ±20 years. Any dotted extension is an extrapolation only.

The dotted segment illustrates continuation of that +10-year divergence only; it is not a prediction.

Illustrative example. The horizontal scale is chronological age. The vertical scale is Bio Age, centred on the client's current chronological age (42) and spanning ±20 years. The pale diagonal means Bio Age equals chronological age. The red measured point at age 42 is Bio Age 52, therefore +10 years older. The dotted line simply carries that same divergence forward until it reaches the top of the displayed Bio Age scale; it does not estimate future health, lifespan or life expectancy.
