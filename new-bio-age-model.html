AFT Composite Biological Age Model

12 markers across body composition, fitness, blood and vital signs. This model is designed to track change in modifiable physiological measures across the Age Reversal Programme.

How it works. Each entered marker is converted to a score of 100, 75, 40 or 10 using the bands below, then multiplied by its agreed model weight. Missing markers are excluded rather than estimated and the remaining weights are automatically re-normalised. The weighted composite is translated into an estimated age difference from chronological age, capped at ±20 years.

Calculation logic

marker score ∈ {100, 75, 40, 10}
weighted composite = Σ(score × weight) / Σ(weights of entered markers)
biological age = chronological age − ((weighted composite − 50) / 50 × 20), capped to chronological age ±20

AFT Bio Age is a programme tracking model, not an epigenetic clock, validated clinical ageing clock, medical diagnosis or lifespan predictor. DEXA, blood testing and VO₂ max are intended for baseline, midpoint and end-of-programme comparison.
