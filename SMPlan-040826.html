Optimal Reel Length (2026 data)

There's no single "default" length — the right duration depends on the job the post is doing:

  • Discovery content (reaching non-followers) — 15–30 seconds. This is Instagram's primary discovery band; shorter Reels get pushed to Explore/the Reels tab far more than longer ones.
  • Educational/explainer content — 30–45 seconds. Enough room to say something real without losing the discovery benefit.
  • 10 Tenets series — fixed at 30–35 seconds (already set, see Tenets tab).
  • Hook in the first 3 seconds matters more than exact length — roughly half of viewers decide to stay or leave in that window, regardless of format.
  • Going much longer than ~90 seconds opts a post out of discovery — it mostly stays inside your existing follower graph instead of reaching new people.

Paid Ads — Meta (Facebook/Instagram)

Critical: paid ads are reviewed under much stricter rules than organic posts. Meta's 2026 health/wellness policy bans "guaranteed" results claims, time-bound transformation claims, and before/after imagery — enforced by semantic AI that catches meaning, not just literal wording. Your organic banner ("REVERSE YOUR BIOLOGICAL AGE BY 10 YEARS. GUARANTEED!") cannot run as paid ad copy as written. This page uses compliant language for ads only — organic brand voice elsewhere is unaffected.

Campaign structure

Trigger to launch: Monday 24 August 2026 — by then you'll have 4 full weeks of the current organic calendar live (27 Jul–15 Aug), giving the retargeting audience below enough engaged profile visitors to actually target. Don't launch paid before this date; cold ads to strangers for a £24k service with no warm audience yet will burn budget for little return.
  • Objective: Leads/Conversions → "Book a consultation," not a direct sale. This is a high-trust, high-ticket service; the ad's job is to start a conversation, not close one.
  • Start with retargeting, not cold traffic: build a Custom Audience from people who've engaged with your Instagram (profile visits, video views, likes/comments) over the last 90 days. Warmer, cheaper, higher intent than cold interest-based targeting — and your best-performing organic posts already exist to build this audience.
  • Trigger point — earliest start date Mon 17 August 2026: that's roughly 4 weeks after this calendar begins (27 July), giving enough organic posting volume to build a real retargeting pool before spending on ads. Don't launch earlier than this; cold ads to strangers for a £24k service with no warm audience yet will burn budget for little return.
  • Geographic targeting: Marbella (Golden Mile, Puerto Banús) as primary; Hove/Brighton affluent postcodes as secondary/bridge test.
  • Naming convention: keep audience and conversion names neutral (e.g. "IG_Engagers_90d," not anything referencing age/health) — health-related audience labels are a flagged pattern in 2026 enforcement.
  • Budget: start small — roughly £15–20/day for an initial 2-week test on the retargeting audience. Scale by 20–30% increments only; large jumps read as risk signals to Meta's AI, independent of ad content.

1. Proof/Authority

Data-led, not narrative. Usually a static carousel, not a talking Reel.
  • DEXA scan: body fat, Body Composition Age context — anchor stat is bio age 44, "nearly two decades younger than chronological age" (triangulated from bloodwork + VO2 Max + DEXA — never use the DEXA-only 48 figure).
  • Next Day Tests bloodwork trend line: the strongest asset here — shows progress over time, not a single snapshot. Crop personal ID details before posting.
  • Whoop Recovery/HRV: screen-recorded, translated into plain terms in the caption.
  • Huawei GT4 readout: paired with the activity it's tracking, not shown in isolation.
  • VO2 Max: label "estimated" until the real lab test is done — currently 50 from device data. Flip to "lab-verified" the moment the real test comes back.

2. Location/Lifestyle

Playful, unscripted, builds Marbella presence. Direction only — not scripted.
  • Same register as the June 25 tequila-shot clip or the June 13 boardwalk clip — light, real, not sales-y.
  • Draws on the Marbella trip B-roll bank, plus ongoing Hove content.
  • No hard pitch — this sub-theme's job is making people want your life, not explaining the programme.
  • Let the moment happen on camera; don't force a message into it.

3. Referral-Partner-Aimed

The one sub-theme not speaking to prospective clients at all.
  • Talks past the client, directly to the doctors/concierges/labs you're courting — "if you work with HNW clients in Marbella..."
  • Explains the finder's-fee mechanics or what makes a good referral fit.
  • Lower frequency than the other two — this is relationship-building with a small, specific audience, not a growth play.
  • Own CTA variant: "DM me the word REFER" rather than the standard "AGE" — keeps referral-partner replies separate from prospective-client replies.

Growth Funnel Strategy — Adapted from @therealbrianmark

📌 Honest framing first: this is a proven content/DM funnel structure, not a guaranteed outcome. Brian's numbers are from his own account, audience size, and price point — his conversion and show-up rates won't automatically transfer to yours. Use his structure, track your own numbers from day one, and adjust.

1. The 3-Tier Content Funnel

Brian's framework organises content into three tiers by funnel position, not randomly mixed:

Funnel TierJobYour Existing Threads
Top — Broad ContentReach anyone, relatable, not niche-lockedThread C (Family Legacy), Thread D-Lifestyle
Middle — Content for Specific Client AvatarSpeaks directly to your actual buyer (HNW, 45+, wants age reversal)Thread A (Tenets), Thread B (What Most People Don't Know), Thread D-Proof
Bottom — Content with DM CTAExplicit call to action, social proof, resultsGap — see below

You don't currently have a dedicated Bottom-of-Funnel format. Once you have client wins (even one), build a simple "results" post type — screenshot-style, direct CTA, no subtlety. This is the tier that actually converts attention into conversations.

2. Automated DM Qualifier (New Tool Recommendation)

Brian's single highest-leverage tactic: every new follower gets an automatic DM asking a qualifying question — not a sales pitch, a simple filter. His example: "Hey fam, thanks for the follow — are you currently fitness coaching, or enjoying the content?"

Your version: "Hey, thanks for following — are you exploring 1:1 age-reversal coaching, or just enjoying the content?" This costs nothing in content-creation time and turns passive followers into active conversations automatically.

Requires a DM automation tool (ManyChat or similar) connected to Instagram. Worth setting up once, running indefinitely.

3. Broadcast Channel

Brian uses an IG Broadcast Channel to share updates directly with followers who've opted in — lower friction than DMs, builds a "insider" relationship layer before the sales conversation. Worth setting up once your following has grown enough to make it worthwhile (Broadcast Channels work best with an engaged base, not a cold one).

4. The Funnel Math — Reverse-Engineered for Your Target

Brian's numbers (his scale): 750 sales calls/month booked → 65% show rate → 487 shows → 45% conversion → 220 new clients/month.

Applying the same ratios to your target of 20 total clients (not monthly — your finite goal):

StageEstimated Volume Needed
New clients (target)20
Sales calls needed (at ~45% close rate)~45
Calls booked needed (at ~65% show rate)~69

The genuinely encouraging takeaway: you don't need anything close to Brian's volume or follower count. ~69 booked calls, spread across your Hove + Marbella push toward April 2027, is a realistic, trackable number — not a mass-audience game. Track your own actual close rate and show rate from your first real calls, and update this table with real data as soon as you have it, rather than relying on Brian's assumed rates.
