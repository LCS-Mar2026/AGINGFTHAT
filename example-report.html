05 · Model

Composite Bio-Age Model

AFT's 12-marker weighted algorithm across body composition, fitness, hormonal, metabolic and vital-sign categories. Divergence capped at ±20 years.

Scoring & colour logic — version 11 September 2026. Domain percentages show each domain's weighting in the AFT Composite Biological Age calculation; they are model weights, not performance scores, and are therefore shown in neutral white. Health-age results are assessed against chronological age: younger is green; more than 5 years younger is Exceptional / bright green; equal is neutral; and older is red. Individual biomarker tiles use the same principle where an age comparison exists; otherwise colour reflects the appropriate performance or reference classification. Exceptional DEXA results, including ALMI at the 99th percentile and a T-score of 3.6, are shown bright green.
