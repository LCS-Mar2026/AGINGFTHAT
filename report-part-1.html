Composite Bio-Age Model · 7 Inputs
HbA1c ★GOOD
33 mmol/molNHS
Reflects 3 month average blood sugar control. Lower is better: high HbA1c accelerates ageing across nearly every organ system via glycation damage.
hsCRP ★GOOD
1.5 mg/LESTIMATED
A marker of systemic inflammation, one of the biggest drivers of accelerated ageing. Not yet tested; shown as a population-average estimate.
Homocysteine ★GOOD
8.0 µmol/LESTIMATED
High levels damage blood vessels and are linked to heart disease and cognitive decline. Not yet tested; shown as a population-average estimate.
Total Testosterone ★GOOD
19.4 nmol/LNHS
Drives muscle mass, bone density, mood and libido. Low levels accelerate muscle loss and cognitive decline with age; higher within range supports vitality.
Free Testosterone ★EXCEPTIONAL
443 pmol/LNHS
The active, usable portion of testosterone. Matters more than the total figure for how you actually feel day to day. Low levels cause fatigue even when total testosterone looks normal.
Systolic BP ★GOOD
120 mmHgGP
Pressure in the arteries when the heart beats. 120 mmHg is a healthy systolic reading and improves the cardiovascular contribution to the AFT Bio Age model.

A strong proxy for cardiovascular fitness. Elite athletes often sit in the 40s; high resting heart rate suggests poor conditioning or stress.
