Your Real Biological Age

Enter your latest biomarker results. The calculator analyses each against optimal ranges and computes your estimated biological age — how old your body actually is, not what your birth certificate says.

ℹ️

How it works: Each biomarker is scored against peer-reviewed optimal ranges. Scores are weighted by their predictive significance for biological ageing. You don't need all values — the calculator adjusts for missing data. More complete data = more accurate result. This is not a medical diagnosis.
