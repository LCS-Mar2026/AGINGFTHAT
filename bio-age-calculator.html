Biological Age Calculator

AFT Composite Biological Age Model · September 2026

What this calculator does. The AFT model combines 12 modifiable markers across DEXA body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness, blood biomarkers and vital signs. Each entered result is scored against defined bands and multiplied by its agreed weight. Missing results are not guessed: they are excluded and the remaining weights are re-normalised. The composite is translated into an estimated biological-age difference, capped at ±20 years. This is an AFT programme-specific composite estimate, not an epigenetic clock, validated clinical ageing clock or medical diagnosis.
